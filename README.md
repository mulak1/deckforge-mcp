## Core Capabilities

1. **8 Business Presentation Templates (100% Free):**
   - Series A Board Update, Q3 Business Review, Customer Proposal & ROI Case, Product Launch & Go-To-Market Plan, Annual Budget & Headcount Plan, M&A / Partnership Evaluation, Crisis & Turnaround Action Plan, Weekly Leadership Standup.
2. **4 Architectural Design Styles & 5 Color Palettes (100% Free):**
   - **Classic Boardroom** (Top Accent Bar & Crisp Square Grid)
   - **Editorial Magazine** (Georgia Serif Typography & Warm Paper Frame)
   - **Modern Minimal** (Rounded Cards & Pill Callout Badges)
   - **Bold Keynote** (High-Contrast Dark Stage & Split Accent Header)
3. **8 Slide Layouts (`+ Add Slide` Picker):**
   - Title & Opening Cover, 2x2 Key Numbers Grid, Quarterly Chart Slide, 3-Pillar Executive Summary, Comparison Table, 3-Phase Roadmap Timeline, Customer Quote & ROI Spotlight, Side-by-Side Comparison.
4. **Spreadsheet / CSV Data Importer & 4 Configurable Chart Types:**
   - Paste or upload `.csv` / `.tsv` / Excel-copied tables and convert them into a **Table Slide** or **Chart Slide** (`vertical_bar`, `horizontal_bar`, `line_trend`, `stacked_bar`) with toggles for Target Line, Value Labels, and Gridlines.
5. **Editable PowerPoint (`.pptx`) & 1-Page Read-Ahead Brief:**
   - Download real OpenXML `.pptx` files with native editable shapes, tables, and speaker notes, plus a 1-Page Meeting Read-Ahead Brief and Deck Readiness Check.
6. **Reviewer Test Credentials:**
   - Free Tier Key: `df_free_demo` (3 presentation exports/month)
   - Unlimited Pro Reviewer Key: `df_pro_enterprise` ($24/month tier unlocked)
